2. Farmer Token Booking Flow & Mechanisms

The token booking workflow (app/farmer/book-slot/page.tsx) enables registered farmers to book a verified slot in four intuitive steps.

2.1 Multi-Crop Selection & Estimated Cost Range

Farmers can schedule up to 3 crops under a single booking token. For each crop:

  • Grade Range Resolution (getCropPriceRange()): Instantly resolves the lowest grade rate (Grade D) and highest grade rate (Grade A). For Wheat, Grade D is ₹2,000/qtl and Grade A is ₹2,425/qtl.
  • Dynamic Rate Banner: Displayed immediately upon selecting the crop: ₹2,000 – ₹2,425 / quintal with individual grade breakdown tags.
  • Individual Crop Payout Preview: Multiplies expected quantity by the rate boundaries: 10 quintals × (₹2,000 – ₹2,425/q) = ₹20,000 – ₹24,250.
  • Combined Total Summary Card: Aggregates quantities and calculates total estimated payout as a min–max range across all chosen produce.

2.2 AI Smart Recommendation Engine

When a farmer chooses their district or location, the backend recommendation engine (/api/recommendations/smart-centre) executes a multi-factor mathematical scoring model:

Recommendation Scoring Formula:
Score = Wdist · (dc / dmax) + Wwait · (wc / wmax) + Wqueue · (qc / qmax)
Where: Wdist = 0.35 (Distance weight), Wwait = 0.45 (Current wait time weight), Wqueue = 0.20 (Active queue congestion weight).
A lower score signifies an optimal procurement terminal with shorter lines, higher bay availability, and lower transit time.

2.3 Guaranteed Unique Token Generation Algorithm

To eliminate duplicate tokens across thousands of daily farmers, lib/token-service.ts implements an atomic, district-partitioned token hashing algorithm:

3. Queue Management & State Machine

Every procurement booking progresses through a strict, auditable 5-stage finite state machine. Transitions are unidirectional and validated by role permissions.

01 WAITING (Scheduled)

Token generated, queue position assigned. Predictive wait time actively counts down. Farmer receives staggered recommended arrival time (10 min prior to slot).

02 CALLED (Gate Arrival)

Farmer arrives at mandi gate. Gate official verifies tractor/vehicle presence and marks "Farmer Arrived", admitting vehicle into the security holding bay.

03 VERIFIED (Docs Cleared)

Official verifies Aadhaar identity, land title (Khasra/Khatauni), bank passbook, and crop declaration. System flags record as green for weighment.

04 PROCESSING (Weigh & Grade)

Vehicle enters weighbridge (gross weight). Produce is sampled for moisture & impurities. Grade A-D assigned. Empty vehicle re-weighed (tare deduction).

05 COMPLETED (DBT Bank Settlement & Form J)

Official authorizes electronic Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) with bank UTR transaction reference. APMC Form J sale voucher is finalized and sealed. Farmer can instantly download, print, or view the J-Slip.

3.1 Predictive Wait Time Engine

Instead of static or inaccurate estimates, MandiMitra calculates wait times dynamically using queue volume and produce weight:

Predictive Wait Time Formula (lib/token-service.ts):
EstimatedWaitMinutes = Math.max(5, Math.round(Nfarmers_ahead · 4 + Qtotal_quintals · 1.2))
Where: 4 minutes represents the fixed inspection & documentation overhead per vehicle, and 1.2 minutes per quintal accounts for weighbridge hydraulic dumping and bag stacking.

3.2 Cancellation & Rescheduling Architecture

Both farmers and mandi officials can cancel a booking prior to weighment:

  • Farmer Self-Cancellation: Available on the tracker and dashboard (/farmer/track-token). Allows farmers to release their slot if delayed due to tractor breakdown or weather.
  • Official Cancellation: Mandi officials can reject a token at the gate or inspection desk with a mandatory reason code (e.g. moisture > 15%, unverified land ownership).
  • Audit Logging: Every cancellation records cancelledAt, cancellationReason, and cancelledBy, preserved in historical archives.

4.3 Statutory APMC Form J-Slip Modal

Under the Agricultural Produce Market Committee Act, farmers must receive an official Form J receipt documenting all transaction particulars:

Contents of the Embedded J-Slip (components/MandiJSlipModal.tsx):
• Header: State APMC Market Committee Name, Mandi Yard License Number, Serial J-Form Number.
• Farmer Particulars: Full Name, Aadhaar Number, Village/Tehsil, Bank Account, and IFSC code.
• Crop & Weight Itemization: Gross Vehicle Weight, Empty Tare Weight, Net Produce Weight (kg & quintals).
• Quality Grade & MSP Rate: Assigned Grade (Grade A–D), Certified MSP rate per quintal, Gross Value.
• Statutory Deductions: Market Cess / Mandi Shulk (1.5%), Rural Development Cess (1.0%).
• Net Farmer Payable (DBT): Final transferred amount in INR numbers and Indian English Words.
• Security Verification: Digital APMC Officer Signature, Official Seal, and Cryptographic QR verification hash.

5. Official Mandi Operations Terminal

The official portal (app/official/procurement/page.tsx & app/official/verify/page.tsx) equips mandi superintendents, weighbridge operators, and grading inspectors with high-throughput operational tooling.

5.1 Gatekeeper & Arrival Verification Desk (/official/verify)

  • Physical Arrival Check: When a vehicle enters the yard gate, the officer verifies the tractor registration and checks the "Farmer Arrived" switch. This instantly updates the status to CALLED and timestamps arrivedAt.
  • Document Verification: Inspects physical documents against the state land portal:
    1. Aadhaar Card (12-digit UID verification)
    2. Land Title / Khasra / Khatauni (Validates declared acres vs expected yield)
    3. Bank Passbook (Confirms active Aadhaar-DBT linking)
    4. Crop Sowing Certificate / Girdawari

5.3 Weighbridge Operations & Automated Payout Calculator

Operators enter certified electronic weighbridge readings. The system automatically computes: 

Net Produce Weight (Quintals) = Gross Vehicle Weight - Tare (Empty) Weight
Farmer Payout = Net Produce Weight × Applied Grade MSP Rate
Total Settlement (Multi-Crop) = Sum(Crop_1_Payout + Crop_2_Payout + Crop_3_Payout)

5.4 1-Click DBT Bank Transfer Disbursal

When the official clicks "Authorize DBT Disbursal", the application executes:

  • Generates a unique banking transaction reference: UTR-DBT-[TOKEN]-[DATE].
  • Marks token status as COMPLETED and paymentStatus: "PAID".
  • Broadcasts the completion event to the farmer's live tracker in real time.
  • Archives the completed sale record into the searchable history store.
